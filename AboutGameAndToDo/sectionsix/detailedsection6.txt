3. DETAILED TECHNICAL AUDIT CRITERIA & INVARIANTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 6.1 Live Command Center & Telemetry:
  * Concurrent Active Users (CCU) real-time counter via Redis session scans.
  * Live tournament stage monitor tracking active participant counts and stage progress.
  * Deposit and withdrawal velocity trackers flagging abnormal volume spikes.
  * System health telemetry: CPU, memory, database connection pool, Redis latency.

- 6.2 Tournament Orchestration & Emergency Controls:
  * Full tournament creation wizard: Title, entry fee, prize pool, registration window, game assignments per stage (1 to 5),
    elimination percentages, and cohort size.
  * Emergency interventions: "Pause Stage", "Extend Timer by X Minutes", "Cancel & Refund All Participants" with transactional
    rollback and automatic entry-fee credit to player wallets.

- 6.3 KYC Verification Desk:
  * Split-screen UI: Document image preview on the left (via private presigned S3 URLs), player details on the right.
  * Action controls: "Approve KYC" (notifies user via SMS/event) and "Reject KYC" (mandatory structured rejection reason).
  * Strict PAN & Aadhaar validation, encryption verification at rest, and masking enforcement.

- 6.4 Fraud & Anti-Cheat Radar:
  * Flagged matches list displaying suspicious move cadences, impossible route timings, or score mismatches.
  * Replay viewer: Visual inspection of player coordinates, timestamp deltas, and standard deviation < 5ms.
  * Account action desk: "Warn Player", "Freeze Wallet", or "Permanent Hardware/Phone Ban" via EnforcementService.

- 6.5 Financial Accounting & Ledger Audit:
  * Complete double-entry ledger explorer verifying zero sum invariants.
  * Breakdown of deposits, withdrawals, platform rake (entry fee - prize pool), and 30% Section 194BA TDS withheld.
  * "Export Tax Sheet (CSV)" generating government-compliant quarterly withholding filings.

- 6.6 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
  * `SUPER_ADMIN`: Unlimited system-wide administrative control.
  * `OPERATIONS_MANAGER`: Tournament creation, scheduling, and stage control.
  * `COMPLIANCE_AGENT`: KYC review, document verification, approval/rejection (strictly no withdrawal access).
  * `FINANCE_CONTROLLER`: Manual withdrawal approvals over ₹10,000, ledger audit, and tax exports.
