2. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & MATHEMATICAL INVARIANTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Cohort Partition Formula:
   - Total registered participants: `N`. Target cohort size: `C = 50 .. 100`.
   - Number of cohorts: `K = Math.max(1, Math.ceil(N / C))`.
   - Assignment: Consistent hash `hash(userId + ":" + tournamentId) % K`.
   - Balanced distribution ensures no cohort exceeds 100 players or drops below 25 players.

B. Percentile Elimination Cut-Off:
   - Given cohort size `M` and advancement rate `P%`:
   - Advancing count: `A = Math.max(1, Math.ceil(M * (P / 100)))`.
   - Bottom `M - A` players are eliminated.
