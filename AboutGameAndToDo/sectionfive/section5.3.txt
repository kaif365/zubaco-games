2. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & MATHEMATICAL INVARIANTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Cohort Partition Formula:
   - Target cohort size: `C = 50 .. 100` (target 75).
   - Number of cohorts: `K = calculateRequiredCohorts(N)`.
   - Deterministic assignment: `computeConsistentCohortShard(userId, tournamentId, K)`.
   - Balanced distribution tested across large populations (statistically uniform across shards).

B. Percentile Elimination Cut-Off:
   - Advancing count: `A = Math.max(1, Math.ceil(M * ((100 - eliminationPct) / 100)))`.
   - Bottom `M - A` players are eliminated.
   - Floor/Ceil guarantee: At least 1 player qualifies in odd-count / small pool boundaries.

C. Anti-Orphan Consolidation:
   - Threshold: `CONSOLIDATION_MIN_THRESHOLD = 25`.
   - Underpopulated cohorts (< 25 entries) automatically merged into adjacent cohorts.
