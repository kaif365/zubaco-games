2. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & MATHEMATICAL INVARIANTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Compound Score Formula:
   - Base game score: `S` (integer).
   - Time elapsed in milliseconds: `T_ms` (0 to 180,000ms for 3-minute max matches).
   - Maximum allowed time: `T_max`. Remaining time: `T_rem = Math.max(0, T_max - T_ms)`.
   - Microsecond decimal component: `D = T_rem / 1,000,000`.
   - `CompoundScore = S + D`.
   - Invariant: A player with higher score ALWAYS beats a lower score (`S1 > S2 => CompoundScore1 > CompoundScore2`).
     If two players have identical base scores (`S1 == S2`), the player who finished faster has higher `T_rem`,
     thus higher `D`, definitively breaking the tie with microsecond precision.

B. Redis Sorted Set Storage:
   - Key: `leaderboard:tournament:${tournamentId}:stage:${stageNumber}:cohort:${cohortId}`
   - Member: `userId`, Score: `CompoundScore`.
