2. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & MATHEMATICAL INVARIANTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Heartbeat Invariant:
   - Ping/pong interval strictly enforced at 5,000ms (`pingInterval: 5000`, `pingTimeout: 5000`).
   - Client disconnect triggers transient disconnection snapshotting.

B. Reconnection Grace Window:
   - Key: `ws:grace:${matchId}:${userId}` with TTL = 30 seconds.
   - Secondary Index: Redis Sorted Set `ws:grace:expirations` with score = `expiresAt`.
   - On reconnect within 30s: Client sends `resume-match` with `matchTicket`; snapshot restored, level & score intact.
   - On expiration: Worker triggers `forfeitSession()`, sets `status = 'FORFEITED'`, locks final score in leaderboard.
