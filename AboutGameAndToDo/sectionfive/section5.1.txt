------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & MATHEMATICAL INVARIANTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Heartbeat Invariant:
   - Ping/pong interval strictly enforced at 5,000ms.
   - 2 missed pings (10 seconds) flags socket as `DISCONNECTED_TRANSIENT`.

B. Reconnection Grace Window:
   - Key: `ws:grace:${matchId}:${userId}` with TTL = 30 seconds.
   - Value: Serialized session snapshot `{ matchId, userId, level, currentScore, lastSeq, disconnectedAt }`.
   - On reconnect within 30s: Client sends `RESUME_SESSION` with `matchTicket`; snapshot restored, timer resumes.
   - On expiration: Worker triggers `FORFEIT_MATCH`, sets `status = 'FORFEITED'`, locks final score in leaderboard.
