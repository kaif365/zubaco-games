WHAT IS ZUBACO?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Zubaco is a real-money, skill-based cognitive gaming platform built for mobile 
and web players. Instead of luck or chance (like slots or lottery), Zubaco hosts 
20 fast-paced, brain-training puzzle games—testing your memory, reaction speed, 
pattern recognition, spatial puzzle solving, logic, and coordination.

Players can practice for free, play casually, or pay an entry fee to compete in 
live tournaments against other real players to win real cash prizes paid out 
directly via UPI or bank transfer.

1. OFFLINE MODE: HOW DOES IT WORK & WHO GENERATES THE LEVELS?
================================================================================
Does Zubaco have an offline mode? YES.

Does the frontend handle levels or do they come from the backend?
The system uses a smart HYBRID approach depending on whether you are playing 
for fun (offline) or playing for real money (online tournament):

A. When Playing OFFLINE (Practice / Free Play Mode):
---------------------------------------------------
- The Frontend handles the levels directly on your phone or browser.
- Every game has its own built-in puzzle generation formula. Even if you turn on 
  Airplane Mode or enter an elevator with zero internet, the game does not stop. 
  It creates new puzzles on the fly, keeps your score, and lets you play level 
  after level.
- Where do your scores go?
  When you are offline, the game saves your progress, stars, and high scores 
  into a local "Outbox" (a digital memory box saved inside your phone).
- When internet returns:
  As soon as your phone gets a Wi-Fi or mobile data signal, the game silently 
  takes all your saved progress from the Outbox and syncs it with the cloud 
  backend. None of your earned stars or unlocked levels are lost!

B. When Playing ONLINE (Tournaments & Real Cash Competitions):
-------------------------------------------------------------
- The Levels COME FROM THE BACKEND (Cloud Server).
- Why? ANTI-CHEAT & FAIR COMPETITION.
  If the frontend generated the puzzle during a cash tournament, a dishonest 
  player could peek into their phone's memory to see the puzzle solution before 
  even making their first move.
- To make it 100% fair, the cloud server creates a "secret puzzle seed" for that 
  match. Both you and your opponents receive identical puzzle layouts generated 
  at the exact same second by the server. Your moves, time, and score are verified 
  by the server to ensure genuine human skill wins every single rupee.

2. TOURNAMENT MODE: HOW DOES IT WORK?
================================================================================
Tournaments are the heart of Zubaco's competitive gameplay. Think of it like 
the "Olympics of Brain Games" with an elimination bracket:

Step 1: Registration Window
---------------------------
- Tournaments appear on the home screen with a countdown timer, entry fee 
  (e.g., ₹10, ₹50, or Free), and a guaranteed prize pool (e.g., ₹10,000).
- Players register using their wallet balance.

Step 2: The 5-Stage Elimination Gauntlet
----------------------------------------
Zubaco tournaments are organized into 5 progressive stages. Each stage tests a 
different cognitive dimension using a specific category of games:

- Stage 1 (Memory): 
  Games like Sequence Recall, Flash Spot, Card Flip, and Object Placement.
  Players memorize patterns and repeat them under time pressure.
- Stage 2 (Sorting & Spatial Skills): 
  Games like Sliding Puzzle, Block Fill, Colour Sort, and Rapid Sort.
  Tests quick spatial awareness and visual sorting.
- Stage 3 (Logic & Navigation): 
  Games like Maze Navigation, Infinity Loop, Word Unscramble, and True/False.
  Tests fast reasoning and deductive thinking.
- Stage 4 (Speed & Eye-Hand Coordination): 
  Games like Arrows, Logic Reflector, Number Grid, and Live Route.
  Tests raw reflexes and split-second precision.
- Stage 5 (The Grand Finale - Endurance & Mastery): 
  Games like Reflex Endurance, Pattern Survival, and Speed Type.
  The hardest challenges reserved for the ultimate finalists.

Step 3: The Cut-Off (Elimination)
---------------------------------
- At the end of each stage, a cut-off threshold applies (for example, the bottom 
  25% of players with the lowest scores are eliminated).
- The remaining players survive and advance to the next stage.

Step 4: Fair Scoring & Cohorts
------------------------------
- To prevent 10,000 players from crashing a single game room, the platform groups 
  players into balanced "Cohorts" (smaller competitor brackets).
- Scoring factors in two things: Accuracy (how many puzzles you got right) and 
  Speed (how many seconds you had left on the clock).

Step 5: Prize Payout
--------------------
- At the end of Stage 5, the winners are ranked on the global leaderboard.
- The prize pool is automatically split among top performers according to the 
  prize distribution table (e.g., 1st gets 40%, 2nd gets 25%, etc.).
- Winnings instantly credit to the player's "Winning Wallet".

3. ADMIN PANEL: WHO CONTROLS THE PLATFORM & WHAT DOES IT DO?
================================================================================
The Zubaco Admin Panel is a secure, private control dashboard used exclusively 
by company operators, game managers, and compliance officers.

It gives the business complete 360-degree control without touching any code:

1. Live Operations Dashboard:
   - Real-time counters showing how many players are currently online, how many 
     matches are active, total entry fees collected today, and total prizes paid.

2. Tournament Manager:
   - Create new tournaments in under 2 minutes.
   - Choose which games are played in which stage.
   - Set the entry fee, max participant cap, prize pool, start date/time, and 
     stage duration.

3. Player Management & Anti-Cheat Oversight:
   - View any player's profile, game history, and match replays.
   - The anti-cheat system automatically flags suspicious behavior (such as 
     superhuman reaction times under 50 milliseconds, bot clicks, or impossible 
     scores).
   - Admins can investigate, freeze accounts, or permanently ban bad actors.

4. KYC & Financial Verification:
   - For real-money withdrawals, Indian laws require player identity verification.
   - Players upload their PAN card / Aadhaar details.
   - Admins review and approve/reject KYC submissions with one click.

5. Ledger & Financial Accounting:
   - Tracks every single rupee moving through the system (Deposits, Entry Fees, 
     Prizes Won, Platform Commission / Rake, and Tax Deductions).
   - Maintains an immutable double-entry ledger so not a single penny is ever lost 
     or unaccounted for.

4. MOBILE: HOW WILL IT BE RENDERED?
================================================================================
How do the 20 rich games display so smoothly on a mobile device?

Zubaco uses a modern, high-performance "Native Shell + Web Bridge" architecture:

1. The Native App Frame (React Native):
   - Handles the smooth phone UI: the bottom navigation bar, splash screen, 
     wallet balance, notifications, login screens, and tournament lists.
   - This native layer feels buttery-smooth (60 to 120 FPS) just like Instagram 
     or Swiggy.

2. The Game Stage (Hardware-Accelerated WebView):
   - When a player taps "Play", the app opens a dedicated full-screen game stage.
   - The game itself is rendered inside an optimized, hardware-accelerated web view.
   - Touch interactions, canvas graphics, and animations render directly using the 
     phone's GPU (graphics chip).

3. The Instant Communication Bridge:
   - The game and the mobile phone talk to each other in real-time via an internal 
     bridge:
     * When you tap a button in the game, the phone vibrates (haptic feedback).
     * Sound effects and background music play through the phone's native sound system.
     * When the match ends, the game whispers the final score to the mobile app, 
       which immediately brings up the tournament result sheet.

5. IS ZUBACO AN APP OR A WEBSITE?
================================================================================
Short Answer: IT IS BOTH! It is a Unified Hybrid Platform.

Here is how the pieces connect:

1. As a Mobile App (Primary Experience for Players):
   - Available as an Android APK (and iOS build).
   - This is what real players download on their phones to play, deposit money, 
     compete in tournaments, and withdraw cash to their bank accounts.

2. As a Web App / Website:
   - Every single one of the 20 games is also a standalone modern web application.
   - Zubaco includes a "Web Lobby" portal that allows anyone to open their web 
     browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge) on a computer, tablet, or phone and test/play 
     any game instantly without installing anything.

3. Why is this hybrid model so powerful?
   - Update games without app store approval: If you improve a level in Maze 
     Navigation or fix a graphic in Sliding Puzzle, you update it on the cloud. 
     Mobile users get the newest version instantly without having to download a 
     heavy 100MB update from the App Store!
   - Ultra-lightweight mobile app: Because the games load dynamically, the initial 
     app download size remains small and quick to install.

6. THE COMPLETE ECOSYSTEM (EVERYTHING ELSE YOU NEED TO KNOW)
================================================================================

A. The 20 Brain-Skill Games:
----------------------------
The platform contains 20 curated skill games grouped by cognitive ability:

  Memory Groups.
- Visual & Spatial: Sliding Puzzle, Block Fill, Colour Sort, Rapid Sort.
- Logic & Problem Solving: Maze Navigation, Infinity Loop, Word Unscramble, 
  True/False Challenge.
- Speed, Reflexes & Dexterity: Arrows, Logic Reflector, Number Grid, Live Route, 
  Reflex Endurance, Pattern Survival, Speed Typing.

B. The Wallet & Real-Money Economy:
-----------------------------------
A player's wallet is split into 3 clear compartments:
1. Deposit Cash: Money the player deposited using UPI / Card / NetBanking.
2. Bonus Cash: Promotional rewards granted by Zubaco (used to discount entry fees).
3. Winning Cash: Real cash won from tournaments and matches. This is the only 
   balance eligible for instant withdrawal.

C. Indian Legal & Tax Compliance:
---------------------------------
1. TDS (Tax Deducted at Source):
   In compliance with Indian financial law, net winnings above the statutory limit 
   automatically have 30% TDS deducted before payouts, and tax receipts are 
   tracked.
2. Geo-Fencing (Location Restrictions):
   Skill gaming for money is legally recognized in most of India, but a few states 
   (such as Assam, Odisha, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Nagaland, and Sikkim) 
   restrict real-money games.
   Zubaco has automatic GPS geo-fencing: if a player opens the app in a restricted 
   state, the real-money tournament tab automatically locks, allowing only free 
   practice games.

D. Anti-Cheat & Fair Play Guarantee:
------------------------------------
- Tap Rate Limiting: Blocks automated clicker tools or scripts.
- Session Heartbeats: Disconnects if connection tampering or memory modification 
  is detected.
- Replay Auditing: Every move timestamp is stored. If a human finishes an 8x8 
  maze in 0.2 seconds, the system immediately flags the impossible time and holds 
  the prize for human review.

E. Audio & Visual Immersion:
----------------------------
- Every game includes a full soundscape: soothing background music, countdown 
  beeps, correct move chimes, error buzzers, and victory fanfare.
- Responsive design: Games automatically stretch and adapt whether played on a 
  tall smartphone (20:9 aspect ratio), an iPad, or a desktop monitor.
