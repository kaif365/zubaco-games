2. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & MATHEMATICAL INVARIANTS CERTIFIED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Section 194BA Statutory Invariants:
   - Rule 133: `Net Winnings = Math.max(0, (Withdrawals + Closing) - (Deposits + Opening))`
   - Rate: Flat 30% (`TDS_RATE = 0.30`).
   - Prior FY Deductions: `TDS Payable = Math.max(0, Total TDS Liability - TDS Already Paid in FY)`.
   - Verified across zero-win, partial withdrawal, full withdrawal, and multiple withdrawal sequences.

B. Geo-Fencing Invariants:
   - Enforces constitutional compliance under state gambling enactments.
   - Dual-layer matching: Both 2-letter ISO 3166-2:IN codes (e.g. `AS`, `OD`, `TS`, `AP`, `SK`, `NL`) and full
     names (`Assam`, `Odisha`, `Telangana`, `Andhra Pradesh`, `Sikkim`, `Nagaland`).
   - Guards both registered user profile states and real-time traveling telemetry (`x-user-state`).
   - Compliant jurisdictions (e.g., Maharashtra, Delhi, Karnataka) are allowed without restriction.
