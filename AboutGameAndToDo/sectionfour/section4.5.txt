2. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & MATHEMATICAL INVARIANTS CERTIFIED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. KYC Withdrawal Gate:
   - In `ledger.service.ts` (`createPendingWithdrawal`), wallet row is locked with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`.
   - Invariant: `if (!w.kyc_verified) throw new BadRequestException('KYC verification required for withdrawals');`.
   - Prevents unverified accounts from withdrawing funds, fulfilling RBI & FIU-IND anti-money-laundering mandates.

B. Data Protection & Cryptography at Rest:
   - Sensitive PAN and Aadhaar identity numbers are encrypted using authenticated `aes-256-gcm`.
   - Ciphertext format: `${iv.hex}:${authTag.hex}:${encrypted.hex}`.
   - Decryption validates authentication tag before returning plaintext.
   - Client APIs return strictly masked identifiers (`ABCDE****F` or `XXXX-XXXX-9012`).

C. Secure S3 Asset Ingress:
   - Direct-to-S3 presigned URLs with HMAC-SHA256 signature and hard 300-second expiration.
   - Prevents public S3 exposure and isolates private identity documents.
