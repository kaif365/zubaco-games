3. DETAILED TECHNICAL AUDIT CRITERIA
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 4.1 Auth & Tokens:
  * SMS Delivery: Indian 10-digit mobile (+91), OTP rate-limiting (max 3 per 10 mins).
  * JWT Architecture: 15-minute access JWT, Redis refresh token rotation, token family revocation upon reuse.

- 4.2 Financial Ledger:
  * 5 Accounts: DEPOSIT_CASH, BONUS_CASH, WINNING_CASH, PLATFORM_RAKE, TDS_ESCROW.
  * Concurrency: `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` row-level locks on user wallet during tournament joins.
  * Race Condition Test: 10 concurrent requests to join ₹50 tournament with ₹50 balance (exactly 1 succeeds, 9 fail).

- 4.3 Webhook Engine:
  * HMAC SHA-256 cryptographic verification of provider webhooks (Razorpay / Cashfree).
  * Idempotency table (`payment_webhook_events`) to discard duplicate deliveries without duplicate wallet credits.

- 4.4 Legal & Tax:
  * Section 194BA 30% TDS calculation: `Net Winnings = (Withdrawals + Closing) - (Deposits + Opening)`.
  * Geofencing: Restrict cash contests in banned states (Assam, Odisha, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Nagaland, Sikkim).

- 4.5 KYC Pipeline:
  * Mandatory threshold before withdrawals; PAN for TDS & Aadhaar for address verification.
  * Presigned private S3 URLs expiring in 5 minutes; sensitive data encrypted at rest.
