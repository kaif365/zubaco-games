2. MANUAL TEST SUITE & STEP-BY-STEP CHECKLIST:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ] TC-01: LETTER TILE DISPLAY & HINT CLUE
    - Action: Start match. Observe jumbled letter tiles and category clue (e.g. "ANIMAL: T - I - G - E - R").
    - Expected Result:
      * Letter tiles are uppercase, large, and contrast well with the card background.
      * Category or definition hint is visible at the top.
      * Empty answer slots correspond to the exact word length.

[ ] TC-02: LETTER SELECTION & ANSWER FILL
    - Action: Tap letters in order: T -> I -> G -> E -> R.
    - Expected Result:
      * Tapped letter animates from the pool up into the first available answer slot.
      * Letter in the pool dims or disappears.
      * Letter tap sound effects play with slight pitch increment per letter.

[ ] TC-03: LETTER DESELECTION & BACKSPACE
    - Action: Tap the letter "G" in the answer slot to remove it, or press "Backspace".
    - Expected Result:
      * Tapped letter slides back into the letter pool tray.
      * Subsequent answer letters shift left or remain in their slots accurately.

[ ] TC-04: CORRECT WORD SUBMISSION & DICTIONARY VALIDATION
    - Action: Complete the correct word "TIGER".
    - Expected Result:
      * Answer row turns bright green with a victory chime.
      * Score awards points based on remaining time and word difficulty.
      * Next word anagram loads instantly.

[ ] TC-05: INCORRECT WORD GUESS
    - Action: Assemble an invalid combination: T -> G -> I -> E -> R and submit.
    - Expected Result:
      * Answer slots shake horizontally in red with an error sound.
      * Letters return to pool or remain selected for player correction.

[ ] TC-06: ALTERNATE VALID ANAGRAMS
    - Action: For words with multiple valid anagrams (e.g. "CARE" vs "RACE"), test submitting either.
    - Expected Result:
      * Recognized dictionary words must be accepted or rejected according to the specific category hint.
