2. MANUAL TEST SUITE & STEP-BY-STEP CHECKLIST:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ] TC-01: INITIAL LOAD & UI ELEMENTS
    - Action: Open the game in Chrome/Safari (Desktop and Mobile Viewport 375x812).
    - Expected Result:
      * 3x3 or 4x4 grid of pads renders centered with equal margins.
      * "Level 1", "Score: 0", "Time / Ready" indicators are clearly legible.
      * Start button is visible and pulsing/highlighted.
      * No overflowing text or layout distortion on mobile view.

[ ] TC-02: DEMONSTRATION PHASE INPUT LOCKOUT
    - Action: Press "Start". Pads begin illuminating in sequence (e.g. 3 pads in Level 1).
    - Intentionally try to click or tap pads while the playback animation is running.
    - Expected Result:
      * Pads MUST completely ignore all click/tap inputs during demonstration.
      * No hover active states or false input registers occur during playback.
      * Pad brightness transitions cleanly (e.g., flash for 600ms then dim).

[ ] TC-03: PLAYER INPUT & CORRECT REPRODUCTION
    - Action: Wait until playback finishes and "Your Turn" indicator appears. Tap the pads in the exact order.
    - Expected Result:
      * Each tapped pad highlights immediately with sound feedback.
      * Input latency feels snappy (< 50ms visual response).
      * After matching the sequence, a success animation plays, score increments, and level advances to Level 2.

[ ] TC-04: INCORRECT INPUT & PENALTY HANDLING
    - Action: During playback of sequence [A, B, C], wait for player turn and intentionally tap [A, D].
    - Expected Result:
      * The wrong pad [D] flashes red with error audio buzzer.
      * Lives decrement or game ends (depending on level rules).
      * Final score screen appears with options to "Retry" or "Submit Score".

[ ] TC-05: HIGH-SPEED LEVEL PROGRESSION (LEVELS 1 TO 10)
    - Action: Play through levels 1 through 10.
    - Expected Result:
      * Sequence length scales smoothly from 3 pads (Level 1) up to 10-12 pads (Level 10).
      * Flash duration scales down proportionally (600ms at Level 1 down to 250ms at Level 10).
      * The randomizer never repeats the identical pad more than twice in a row.

[ ] TC-06: RAPID SPAM / MULTI-TOUCH TEST
    - Action: Use 3 fingers to spam all 9 pads simultaneously when "Your Turn" starts.
    - Expected Result:
      * Game engine handles multi-touch gracefully without crashing or throwing JavaScript console errors.
      * Erroneous tap triggers game over immediately and cleanly.

[ ] TC-07: AUDIO UNLOCK & MUTE TOGGLE
    - Action: Click mute button in top corner. Play a sequence. Unmute.
    - Expected Result:
      * Mute silences all sound effects instantly.
      * Unmute restores sound on the next pad tap.
      * Audio initializes cleanly without Safari/iOS autoplay blockage warnings.

[ ] TC-08: OUTBOX OFFLINE DISCONNECT TEST
    - Action: Open DevTools -> Network Tab -> Toggle to "Offline". Complete a level.
    - Expected Result:
      * Game does not crash.
      * Score payload is queued in local outbox (IndexedDB/localStorage).
      * Toggle Network back to "Online" -> Outbox flushes score cleanly without duplicates.
