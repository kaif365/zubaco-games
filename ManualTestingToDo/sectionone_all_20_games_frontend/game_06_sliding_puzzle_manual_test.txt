2. MANUAL TEST SUITE & STEP-BY-STEP CHECKLIST:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ] TC-01: MATHEMATICAL SOLVABILITY INVARIANT (CRITICAL P0)
    - Action: Generate 10 randomized boards in a row (3x3 grid = 8 tiles + 1 blank).
    - Expected Result:
      * Every single generated board MUST be mathematically solvable.
      * Inversion count $N = \sum \text{inversions}$ must be strictly EVEN for 3x3 grids.
      * At no point should an unsolvable permutation (e.g. 14-15 swap parity bug) appear.

[ ] TC-02: ADJACENT TILE SLIDE ACTION
    - Action: Tap a tile that is immediately adjacent (up, down, left, right) to the empty blank slot.
    - Expected Result:
      * Tile smoothly slides into the empty space with CSS transform / transition.
      * Empty space moves to the tile's previous coordinate.
      * Move counter increments by exactly 1.
      * Soft sliding click sound plays.

[ ] TC-03: NON-ADJACENT TILE TAP (ILLEGAL MOVE)
    - Action: Tap a corner tile that is NOT adjacent to the empty slot.
    - Expected Result:
      * Tile must NOT move.
      * No visual teleportation or state corruption.
      * Invalid move does NOT increment move counter or penalize the player.

[ ] TC-04: SWIPE GESTURE SUPPORT ON TOUCH DEVICES
    - Action: On a mobile device / touch emulator, swipe toward the empty slot.
    - Expected Result:
      * Tile responds to swipe direction and slides into the blank.
      * Gesture threshold prevents accidental moves during tiny screen drags.

[ ] TC-05: WIN CONDITION TRIGGER & FINAL PIECE REVEAL
    - Action: Arrange tiles in order: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.
    - Expected Result:
      * The missing 9th tile / picture piece animates into the empty space to complete the picture/grid.
      * Win banner appears with move count and completion time.
      * Score accurately awards bonuses for fewer moves and faster time.

[ ] TC-06: RAPID SLIDING INPUT RUSH
    - Action: Rapidly tap 5 adjacent tiles in quick succession (under 500ms).
    - Expected Result:
      * Tile animations queue or complete sequentially without overlapping tiles or duplicate tile numbers.
