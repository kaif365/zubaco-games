2. MANUAL TEST SUITE & STEP-BY-STEP CHECKLIST:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ] TC-01: GROUP DISPLAY & THEMATIC CATEGORIES
    - Action: Start match. Observe items belonging to distinct categories (e.g., Fruits, Animals, Vehicles).
    - Expected Result:
      * Items have clear icons and labels (e.g. Apple, Banana, Orange vs Dog, Cat, Horse).
      * Category headers or drop zones are visibly defined at the top.
      * Cards are randomized across the staging area.

[ ] TC-02: SELECTING AND GROUPING ITEMS
    - Action: Tap an item and tap its corresponding category zone (or drag into container).
    - Expected Result:
      * Item animates smoothly into the group cluster.
      * Category counter increments (e.g., "Fruits: 1/3").
      * Correct placement plays a soft affirmative chime.

[ ] TC-03: MISCLASSIFICATION HANDLING
    - Action: Intentionally drag an Animal (Dog) into the Fruit category.
    - Expected Result:
      * Category box rejects item with horizontal shake animation and error buzz.
      * Item returns to staging area.
      * Mistake counter increments or score is penalized.

[ ] TC-04: COMPLETED GROUP CELEBRATION
    - Action: Successfully assign all items of a single category.
    - Expected Result:
      * Category box seals with a golden border and checkmark.
      * Remaining items rearrange to give more space for unsolved categories.

[ ] TC-05: ADVANCED DIFFICULTY (LEVELS 1 TO 10)
    - Action: Advance to Level 5 and Level 8.
    - Expected Result:
      * Number of categories increases from 2 (Level 1) to 4 (Level 8).
      * Categorical nuance increases (e.g., Mammals vs Reptiles, Musical Instruments vs Tools).
      * No item belongs to multiple categories simultaneously (unambiguous taxonomy).

[ ] TC-06: ACCIDENTAL RAPID TAPPING
    - Action: Rapidly tap multiple items simultaneously.
    - Expected Result:
      * No items overlap, clip through containers, or disappear from the DOM.
