[ ] TC-02: PIECE DRAG AND SNAP TO GRID

    - Expected Result:
      * Ghost highlight shows valid landing cells under the piece while dragging.

      * Snapped cells change to the piece color and play a snap sound.

[ ] TC-03: INVALID PLACEMENT COLLISION

      * Upon release, piece snaps back to the tray without placing.
      * No cells are corrupted or partially filled.

[ ] TC-04: PIECE REMOVAL / PICKUP
    - Action: Tap or drag an already placed piece out of the grid back into the tray.
    - Expected Result:
      * Grid cells under the piece clear back to empty state.
      * Piece returns to tray ready for re-placement.

[ ] TC-05: FULL BOARD FILL WIN TRIGGER
    - Action: Place all pieces so 100% of grid cells are filled with zero gaps.
    - Expected Result:
