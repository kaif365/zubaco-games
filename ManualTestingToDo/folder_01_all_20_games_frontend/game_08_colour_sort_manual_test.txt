2. MANUAL TEST SUITE & STEP-BY-STEP CHECKLIST:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ] TC-01: TUBE CAPACITY & COLOR DISTRIBUTION
    - Action: Launch game. Observe glass test tubes containing layered color blocks/liquids.
    - Expected Result:
      * Standard tubes have exactly 4 segments capacity.
      * At least 2 empty buffer tubes are present to allow valid transfer moves.
      * Every color present on the board has exactly 4 segments total across all tubes.
      * The initial board is mathematically proven solvable.

[ ] TC-02: SELECT TUBE & POUR ANIMATION
    - Action: Tap Tube A (source), then tap Tube B (destination with matching top color or empty).
    - Expected Result:
      * Tube A elevates slightly or tilts to indicate selection.
      * Top matching liquid block pours smoothly from Tube A into Tube B with a liquid pouring sound effect.
      * Tube A drops back down to the baseline.

[ ] TC-03: ILLEGAL POUR REJECTION (COLOR MISMATCH)
    - Action: Select Tube A with Red on top, and tap Tube B with Blue on top.
    - Expected Result:
      * Pour is rejected.
      * Tube B shakes horizontally with a dull refusal thud.
      * Tube A remains selected or deselects cleanly.

[ ] TC-04: ILLEGAL POUR REJECTION (FULL TUBE)
    - Action: Select Tube A and try to pour into Tube B which already has 4 segments (full).
    - Expected Result:
      * Pour is strictly rejected.
      * No overflow graphic or liquid clipping outside tube glass.

[ ] TC-05: CONSECUTIVE SAME-COLOR TRANSFER
    - Action: Pour from a tube having 2 consecutive segments of Green into an empty tube.
    - Expected Result:
      * Both consecutive segments of the same color pour together or sequentially without requiring 2 taps.

[ ] TC-06: TUBE COMPLETION LOCK
    - Action: Fill a tube with 4 identical color segments (e.g., 4 Yellow).
    - Expected Result:
      * Tube caps off with a cork or golden sparkler effect.
      * Tube is sealed and cannot be selected as source anymore.

[ ] TC-07: LEVEL COMPLETE TRIGGER
    - Action: Complete all tubes so every tube is either full of a single color or completely empty.
    - Expected Result:
      * Win fanfare plays, fireworks or confetti trigger on canvas.
      * Move count and timer bonus display on summary screen.
