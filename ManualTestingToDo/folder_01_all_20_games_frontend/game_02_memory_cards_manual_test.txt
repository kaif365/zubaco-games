2. MANUAL TEST SUITE & STEP-BY-STEP CHECKLIST:
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ ] TC-01: GRID LAYOUT & CARD BACKS
    - Action: Launch game on mobile screen (375x812) and tablet (768x1024).
    - Expected Result:
      * Grid is symmetrical (e.g. 4x3 in Level 1 = 12 cards, 6 pairs).
      * Card backs have consistent graphics with no visual tearing.
      * Cards are sized proportionally so none are clipped at screen edges.

[ ] TC-02: INITIAL PEEK / MEMORIZATION PHASE
    - Action: Start match. Observe initial 2-second card reveal.
    - Expected Result:
      * All cards flip face up for exactly 2 seconds, displaying distinct icons/symbols.
      * Cards flip face down smoothly with 3D flip animation.
      * Clicking during the peek phase is locked out.

[ ] TC-03: MATCHING PAIR BEHAVIOR
    - Action: Click Card A (e.g. Lion), then click Card B (Lion).
    - Expected Result:
      * Both cards flip face up.
      * On match detection, a green success glow and celebratory chime sound plays.
      * The matched pair remains face up and is permanently disabled from future clicks.
      * Match counter increments (+1 pair matched).

[ ] TC-04: MISMATCH PAIR BEHAVIOR & FLIP-BACK DELAY
    - Action: Click Card A (Lion), then click Card C (Tiger).
    - Expected Result:
      * Both cards remain visible face up for exactly 800ms - 1000ms so player can memorize them.
      * Both cards smoothly flip back face down.
      * Move counter increments by 1.

[ ] TC-05: MULTI-CLICK LOCKOUT (EDGE CASE)
    - Action: Rapidly click 4 different cards in under 300ms.
    - Expected Result:
      * Engine strictly restricts visible flips to 2 cards at any one time.
      * The 3rd and 4th clicks must NOT reveal cards while the mismatch animation is playing.
      * No stuck face-up cards or Desync states occur.

[ ] TC-06: SAME-CARD DOUBLE-CLICK
    - Action: Click Card A once, and immediately double-click Card A again.
    - Expected Result:
      * Card A must NOT match with itself.
      * The second click on an already revealed card must be ignored.

[ ] TC-07: LEVEL ADVANCEMENT & BOARD SCALING
    - Action: Complete all pairs on the board.
    - Expected Result:
      * Win screen triggers with remaining time bonus calculated.
      * Next level advances to higher card count (e.g. 4x4 = 16 cards, 4x5 = 20 cards).
      * Timer scales appropriately for the increased cognitive load.

[ ] TC-08: TIMEOUT EXPIRATION
    - Action: Let the countdown timer expire with unmatched cards remaining.
    - Expected Result:
      * Timer reaches 0:00. Game immediately freezes input.
      * Game Over dialog appears with final score based on pairs cleared.
