[ ] TC-01: NET WINNINGS 30% TDS FORMULA VERIFICATION (INDIAN INCOME TAX COMPLIANCE)
    - Action: User requests withdrawal of ₹1,000.
      * Financial Year Total Deposits: ₹2,000
      * Financial Year Total Withdrawals to Date: ₹1,500
      * Net Winnings Calculation:
        $\text{Net Winnings} = (\text{Total Withdrawals} + \text{Current Withdrawal}) - \text{Total Deposits}$
        $\text{Net Winnings} = (₹1,500 + ₹1,000) - ₹2,000 = ₹500$
      * TDS Due (30% on Net Winnings): $30\% \times ₹500 = ₹150$
      * Net Payout Dispatched to Bank: $₹1,000 - ₹150 = ₹850$
    - Expected Result:
      * Backend computes exactly ₹150 TDS.
      * ₹150 TDS credited to Government TDS withholding ledger account.
      * User receives ₹850 in UPI and TDS certificate details appear in their withdrawal history.
