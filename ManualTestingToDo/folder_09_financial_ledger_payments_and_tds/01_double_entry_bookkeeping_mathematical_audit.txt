[ ] TC-01: MATHEMATICAL BALANCE INVARIANT CHECK ($\sum \text{DEBITS} \equiv \sum \text{CREDITS}$)
    - Action: Run manual database SQL query:
      ```sql
      SELECT 
        SUM(CASE WHEN direction = 'DEBIT' THEN amount ELSE 0 END) AS total_debits,
        SUM(CASE WHEN direction = 'CREDIT' THEN amount ELSE 0 END) AS total_credits,
        SUM(CASE WHEN direction = 'DEBIT' THEN amount ELSE -amount END) AS variance
      FROM "WalletLedgerEntry";
      ```
    - Expected Result:
      * `variance` MUST BE STRICTLY `0.00` at all times.
      * No floating-point rounding errors (amounts stored in Integer Paise: ₹10.00 = 1000 paise).

[ ] TC-02: TOURNAMENT RAKE ALLOCATION VERIFICATION
    - Action: 16 players join ₹50 tournament. Total entry pool = ₹800.
      * Platform Rake (e.g. 15%): $₹800 \times 15\% = ₹120$
      * Guaranteed Prize Pool: $₹800 - ₹120 = ₹680$
    - Expected Result:
      * Ledger records debit of ₹50 from 16 players (Total Debits: ₹800).
      * Ledger records credit to Platform Rake Revenue Account: ₹120.
      * Ledger records credit to Tournament Escrow Account: ₹680.
      * Total debits (₹800) === Total credits (₹120 + ₹680 = ₹800).

[ ] TC-03: BONUS BUCKET EXPIRATION / FORFEITURE TEST
    - Action: Expire a user's ₹50 promotional bonus cash via scheduled job.
    - Expected Result:
      * User's `bonusBalance` decrements by ₹50.
      * Offsetting entry credits the "Company Bonus Reserve Account".
      * User's `depositBalance` and `winningBalance` remain 100% untouched.

[ ] TC-04: CONCURRENT TOURNAMENT CANCELLATION RUSH
    - Action: 50 players registered in a tournament. Force-cancel the tournament.
    - Expected Result:
      * All 50 players receive exact refund entries.
      * Every player's original deducted buckets (e.g. ₹10 bonus + ₹40 deposit) are refunded to their original buckets.
      * No bonus cash is accidentally converted to real withdrawable cash during refunds.
