1) FINAL VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOT PRODUCTION-READY.

Maze generation is provably fair and server-authoritative: mazes are carved with
a mulberry32 seeded DFS keyed on finalSeed = sha256(serverSeed:clientSeed:nonce),
serverSeed via crypto.randomUUID, guaranteeing solvable single-solution mazes;
BFS computes the true shortest path; moves are validated server-side
(validateMazeMove: bounds + wall reject), future-timestamp skew is rejected, and
score is bounded (round 100–1000 efficiency, time bonus capped 500). Strong core.
Blocking: which template/maze a player gets is chosen with Math.random (fairness),
the WS gateway is the same inherited insecure boilerplate, and submit-moves
moves[] has no max cap (DoS).
