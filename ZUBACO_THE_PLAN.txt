 1. WHAT THE GAME IS (concept, in one breath)
================================================================================
Shunya = a mass-participation, skill-based ELIMINATION TOURNAMENT.
Millions register -> play 5 ONLINE stages (4 games each) -> OFFLINE finale ->
ONE champion.
  - Stages 1-3 (PRE-BUCKETING): players grouped into WEEKLY cohorts ("buckets")
    by registration week. Registration closes after week 5/6. ~50% eliminated
    per stage, WITHIN each bucket.
  - Stages 4-5 (POST-BUCKETING): all survivors MERGE into one unified pool.
  - Finale: last players compete offline -> 1 winner.
  - The CLIENT IS NEVER TRUSTED. All scoring/validation is server-side.
